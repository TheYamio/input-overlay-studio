> **A redesigned Studio workspace with on-canvas editing, custom SVG shapes, new motion and lighting effects, modular settings, and 12 experimental interface languages.**

### Highlights
- **New Studio layout.** More room for your composition, resizable properties panel and improved scrolling on smaller windows.
- **Direct canvas editing.** Resize handles, keyboard shortcuts and precise positioning for modules and individual keys.
- **Creative tools.** Import SVG shapes, customize keyboard and mouse artwork, and apply preset palettes without changing layout.
- **Animation control.** Separate movement and lighting effects, gradients, easing, delays, and press/release durations.
- **More languages.** Choose from 12 experimental interface translations.

**Updating from v0.4.0?** Its saved profiles cannot be imported into this version. Keep a backup before updating.

### Install in OBS
1. [Download the Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/TheYamio/input-overlay-studio/releases/download/v0.6.2/Input-Overlay-Studio-0.6.2-Windows-x64.zip) and **extract the entire archive**.
2. Run `Input Overlay Studio.exe` and keep the bundled DLLs next to it. [Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/) is required.
3. In Studio select **Copy OBS URL**. In OBS create a **Browser Source** at **1920 × 1080** and paste the URL. The transparent overlay can keep running after the editor window closes.
